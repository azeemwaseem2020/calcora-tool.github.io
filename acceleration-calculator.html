Acceleration Calculator

Calculate average acceleration from initial velocity, final velocity and elapsed time.

Enter velocity and time

Acceleration formula

a = (v − u) ÷ t, where u is initial velocity, v is final velocity and t is elapsed time. With velocity in m/s and time in seconds, acceleration is measured in m/s².

Worked example

If velocity changes from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 seconds: a = (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².

How to calculate acceleration

  1. Enter the initial velocity.
  2. Enter the final velocity.
  3. Enter elapsed time greater than zero.
  4. Subtract initial velocity from final velocity and divide by time.

Understanding the result

A positive result means velocity increased in the chosen direction. A negative result means velocity decreased under the chosen sign convention. This calculator gives average acceleration over the selected time interval.

Unit consistency

Use compatible units before calculating. For example, convert kilometres per hour to metres per second if you need acceleration in m/s².

Limitations

This simple tool does not model changing acceleration within the interval, forces, friction or detailed motion. It uses the standard average-acceleration formula.
