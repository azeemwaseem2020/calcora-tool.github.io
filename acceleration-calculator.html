Acceleration Calculator

Calculate acceleration from initial velocity, final velocity and elapsed time.

Formula

a = (v − u) ÷ t, where u is initial velocity, v is final velocity and t is elapsed time. The result is metres per second squared (m/s²).

Worked example

If velocity changes from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 seconds: a = (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s².

Calculation steps

  1. Enter the initial velocity.
  2. Enter the final velocity.
  3. Enter elapsed time greater than zero.
  4. Subtract initial velocity from final velocity and divide by time.

Interpreting the result

A positive result indicates increasing velocity in the chosen direction; a negative result indicates decreasing velocity under the sign convention used. This simple calculator assumes constant average acceleration over the interval.
