5KW SOLAR SYSTEM GUIDE

5kW Solar System Calculator: Panels, Inverter, Battery & Roof Area

A 5kW label tells you the nominal PV or inverter capacity; it does not automatically tell you the correct panel count, battery size, roof area or expected bill savings. Those depend on the load, equipment and site.

Planning rule: use a 5kW calculation as a first-pass estimate. Final equipment selection should be checked against actual electricity use, module and inverter specifications, site conditions and applicable local requirements.

What does 5kW mean?

In solar discussions, 5kW may refer to the nominal capacity of the PV array or the AC output rating of an inverter. Those are not necessarily identical. Always check which rating a quotation is describing before comparing systems.

How many panels make 5kW?

The simple panel-count calculation is target watts ÷ panel watts, rounded up. With 550W modules, 5,000 ÷ 550 = 9.09, so 10 panels would provide about 5.5kW of nominal panel capacity. A real array may be designed differently to match the inverter's permitted DC input, string voltage and the module's electrical characteristics.

How much energy can a 5kW system produce?

A first-pass daily estimate is system capacity × peak sun hours × assumed system efficiency. For example, at 5 peak sun hours and 80% overall efficiency, 5 × 5 × 0.8 = about 20kWh per day. A monthly first-pass estimate would multiply the daily estimate by the number of days in the month.

This is not guaranteed production. Season, heat, dust, shading, orientation, inverter clipping, wiring losses, module condition and weather can change actual output. Use local production data or a professional site assessment for a final design.

How do I choose a 5kW inverter?

Start with the intended AC loads and the PV array, then check continuous output, surge capability, PV voltage and current limits, MPPT range, battery compatibility and grid requirements. Do not assume that a 5kW PV array automatically requires an identical inverter rating.

How much battery backup do I need?

Battery size is an energy question, not simply a copy of the solar-system kW rating. Estimate the essential load in watts, decide how many hours you want backup, and account for usable battery capacity and conversion losses. For example, 1,000W of essential load for 5 hours represents 5kWh of energy before additional design margins and losses.

How much roof area is needed?

Estimate panel area from panel count × physical panel area, then allow additional room for mounting, access, setbacks and obstructions. If 10 panels each occupy 2.3m², the panel faces alone occupy about 23m²; the usable roof area normally needs to be larger.

What determines whether 5kW is suitable?

Energy use

Review recent electricity consumption in kWh rather than choosing a system from appliance wattage alone.

Daytime demand

Consider how much electricity is used while solar production is available.

Backup goal

Decide which essential loads must run during outages before selecting battery and inverter capacity.

Roof constraints

Check shade, orientation, structural limits, access paths and usable mounting area.

Planning for Pakistan

For a Pakistan installation, local solar conditions, summer heat, dust, roof orientation, shading, electricity usage and selected equipment all affect results. Grid-connected and backup systems also have different design requirements. Treat the calculator as an estimate and have the final electrical design checked against applicable requirements and manufacturer specifications.

Common 5kW solar planning mistakes

  • Assuming a 5kW label guarantees a fixed daily energy output.
  • Choosing panel count without checking inverter DC limits and string design.
  • Sizing a battery from the solar kW number instead of essential-load energy.
  • Ignoring roof access, shading and mounting space.
  • Using an electricity bill from an unusual month as the only load estimate.
  • Comparing quotations without checking whether each supplier means PV capacity or inverter capacity.

Frequently asked questions

Can 10 × 550W panels be called a 5kW system?

They provide about 5.5kW of nominal panel capacity. Whether the complete system is described as a 5kW system depends on the inverter and the supplier's naming convention.

How much electricity does 5kW solar produce per day?

There is no single guaranteed number. A simple estimate uses system capacity, peak sun hours and an efficiency assumption. Actual production changes with location, season, weather, temperature, shading and equipment.

Does 5kW solar mean I need a 5kWh battery?

No. Solar capacity is measured in kW while battery storage is usually discussed in kWh. Battery size should follow the energy and duration of the loads you want to back up.

Will a 5kW system eliminate my electricity bill?

Not necessarily. The result depends on consumption, solar production, self-consumption, export arrangements, tariffs, fixed charges and system configuration.
