5KW SOLAR SYSTEM GUIDE

5kW Solar System Calculator: Panels, Inverter, Battery & Roof Area

A 5kW label tells you the nominal PV or inverter capacity; it does not automatically tell you the correct panel count, battery or bill savings.

How many panels make 5kW?

The panel count is target watts divided by panel watts, rounded up. With 550W modules, 5,000 ÷ 550 gives 9.09, so 10 panels would be needed to reach at least 5kW using that simple rule. A real array may be designed differently to match the inverter's permitted DC input and the selected module's electrical characteristics.

How much energy can 5kW produce?

Production depends on solar resource and system losses. A simple estimate is 5 kW × peak sun hours × efficiency per day. Multiply by the number of days for a rough monthly figure. Seasonal weather, heat, dust, shading, orientation and equipment can move the actual result.

Does a 5kW system need a 5kW battery?

No. Battery size should follow the energy you want stored and the hours of backup you need. A small essential-load backup can require far less storage than running the entire home for several hours.

Roof area

Estimate panel area from panel count × physical panel area, then add space for access, mounting and obstructions. Do not treat the raw panel-area result as the exact usable roof requirement.

Who should consider a 5kW system?

It can suit a household or small site with moderate electricity use, but the correct size should come from actual kWh consumption, daytime load, future demand and the system's operating goal.
