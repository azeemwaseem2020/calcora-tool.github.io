PSX Average Price Calculator

Find the weighted average cost of your shares after adding a new purchase at a different price.

Average price formula

New average price = (existing shares × existing average price + new shares × new purchase price) ÷ total shares. This is a weighted average, not a simple average of the two prices.

Example

Buying 1,000 shares at PKR 100 and another 500 at PKR 80 gives a weighted average of PKR 93.33 before any additional transaction costs.

PSX costs and taxes

Brokerage, transaction charges and taxes can affect your true cost basis or realized return. Enter actual costs in a broader trade analysis and verify current rules with your broker or applicable official sources.
