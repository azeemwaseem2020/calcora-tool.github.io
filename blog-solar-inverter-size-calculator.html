SOLAR INVERTER GUIDE

Solar Inverter Size Calculator: How to Choose kW or kVA

The right inverter is determined by the loads it must run and the electrical limits of the complete solar system.

Start here: add the maximum continuous AC load, then check motor or compressor surge, power factor, inverter efficiency, PV input limits, MPPT range, battery compatibility and the intended grid configuration.

Worked example

Suppose the appliances you expect to run together have a continuous load of 3,200 W. A first-pass calculation is 3.2 kW of running demand. If one motor-driven appliance has a significant starting surge, the inverter must also have enough surge capability to start it. The final model should therefore be selected from its published continuous and surge ratings, not from the 3.2 kW figure alone.

Why watts alone are not enough

A refrigerator, pump or air conditioner can draw a short starting surge above its normal running power. An inverter that looks large enough from average watts may still trip if its surge capability is insufficient.

kW and kVA

kW measures real power. kVA represents apparent power and becomes important when equipment has a power factor below 1. The relationship depends on power factor, so do not convert kW to kVA with a universal fixed number without checking the equipment and inverter specifications.

Check the solar side too

The inverter must accept the planned PV array's DC voltage and current and operate within its MPPT window. A larger PV array can sometimes be paired with a smaller AC inverter under a manufacturer's permitted design ratio, but this is a technical design decision rather than a generic sizing rule.

Hybrid and backup systems

If the inverter will provide backup, confirm its battery voltage range, maximum charge and discharge current, supported battery type and maximum backup output. Also identify which circuits are essential during an outage. Battery energy affects runtime, while inverter power affects which loads can run at the same time.

Before choosing an inverter

Running load

Calculate the maximum normal load you expect to operate together.

Starting surge

Check pumps, compressors, motors and other loads with high startup demand.

System limits

Verify PV voltage/current, MPPT range, battery compatibility and backup rating.
