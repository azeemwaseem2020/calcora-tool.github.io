Solar Inverter Size Calculator: How to Choose kW or kVA

The right inverter is determined by the loads it must run and the electrical limits of the complete solar system.

Quick rule: start with the maximum continuous AC load, then check motor/compressor surge, power factor, inverter efficiency, PV input limits, MPPT range, battery compatibility and the intended grid configuration.

Why watts alone are not enough

A refrigerator, pump or air conditioner can have a short starting surge above its normal running power. An inverter that looks large enough from average watts may still trip if its surge capability is insufficient.

kW and kVA

kW measures real power. kVA represents apparent power and becomes important when equipment has a power factor below 1. The relationship depends on power factor, so do not convert kW to kVA with a universal fixed number without knowing the equipment and inverter specifications.

Check the solar side too

The inverter must accept the planned PV array's DC voltage and current and operate within its MPPT window. A larger PV array can sometimes be paired with a smaller AC inverter under a manufacturer's permitted design ratio, but this is a technical design decision, not a generic rule.

Hybrid and backup systems

If the inverter will provide backup, confirm its battery voltage range, maximum charge/discharge current, supported battery type and maximum backup output. Also identify which circuits are essential during an outage.
