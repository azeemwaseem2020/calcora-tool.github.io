How to Estimate Solar Panel Size

A basic solar estimate starts with daily energy use, peak sun hours and system efficiency.

Start with daily energy

For a simple load estimate, multiply appliance power in watts by hours used each day and divide by 1,000 to get kilowatt-hours.

Why efficiency matters

Real systems have losses. A planning estimate can divide daily energy demand by peak sun hours and an assumed system efficiency to produce a starting capacity in kilowatts.

This is not a final engineering design; roof conditions, shading, battery needs, local weather and equipment specifications should also be assessed.
