How to Turn Electricity Use Into a Solar Size Estimate

A useful first step in solar planning is understanding how much energy your appliances consume each day.

Start with watts and hours

Multiply an appliance or combined load in watts by the number of hours it runs each day. Divide by 1,000 to express the result in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

Example: a 1,000-watt load used for 5 hours represents about 5 kWh of daily energy use before system losses.

Why sun hours and efficiency matter

Solar output varies with location, season, orientation, shading, temperature and equipment losses. A basic planning estimate divides daily energy demand by peak sun hours and an assumed efficiency factor.

What a calculator cannot decide for you

A final design needs more information: peak loads, inverter capacity, battery requirements, roof conditions, backup goals and local solar resource. The calculator is a starting point for discussion, not an installation design.
