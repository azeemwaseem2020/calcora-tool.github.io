How Compound Interest Works

Compound interest allows earned interest to become part of the balance that can earn further interest.

The basic idea

Growth depends on the starting amount, interest rate, time and how often interest compounds. Over longer periods, compounding can make a meaningful difference compared with simple interest.

Use realistic assumptions

Calculator results are estimates. Actual savings and investment returns can vary because rates, deposits, withdrawals, fees and taxes may change.
