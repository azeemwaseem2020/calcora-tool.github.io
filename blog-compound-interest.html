Compound Interest: Why Time Matters More Than It Seems

Compounding turns the passage of time into an important part of a growth calculation.

The basic idea

With simple interest, interest is calculated on the original principal. With compound interest, earned interest is added to the balance and can then participate in future growth.

What changes the result?

Starting amount

A larger principal gives the calculation a larger base.

Rate

The annual return or interest assumption directly affects growth.

Time

More periods give compounding more opportunities to work.

Compounding frequency

Monthly, quarterly and annual compounding can produce different results even when the quoted annual rate is the same. Always check how the rate is defined before comparing products.

What this estimate leaves out

Taxes, fees, inflation, withdrawals and future deposits can materially change a real investment outcome. Treat a calculator result as a scenario rather than a promise.
