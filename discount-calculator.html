Discount Calculator

Calculate your discount amount, savings and final sale price from an original price and discount percentage.

Discount formula

Discount amount = Original price × Discount rate ÷ 100

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount

Worked example

For a price of 10,000 with a 20% discount, savings are 2,000 and the final sale price is 8,000.

Where a discount calculator helps

Use it for sale prices, shopping discounts, marked-down products, promotional offers and quick price comparisons. If a seller applies tax, shipping or another fee after the discount, calculate that additional cost separately.

Discount vs percentage change

A discount reduces an original price by a stated percentage. A percentage-change calculation can instead compare an old value with a new value. These calculations answer different questions, so use the appropriate tool.
