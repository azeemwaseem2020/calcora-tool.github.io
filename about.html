About Calcora

Calcora is a lightweight collection of practical online tools. The goal is to make common digital tasks easier with clear, fast and mobile-friendly web pages.

Our approach

We focus on useful features, straightforward instructions and accessible interfaces. Browser-based tools are designed to keep supported processing on your device where practical.

Tools

Our first public utility is an image resizing tool, with more tools planned for future releases.
