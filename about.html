ABOUT CALCORA

Practical calculations, clearly explained

Calcora is a free online calculator project focused on useful everyday planning. Our tools cover finance, solar energy and agriculture, with simple inputs and readable results.

Our approach: give users a quick estimate while explaining the underlying calculation and its practical limitations.

What Calcora aims to provide

Fast pages, mobile-friendly controls, transparent formulas and useful educational guides. No account is required to use the calculators.

Accuracy and responsible use

Calculator results are estimates based on user-entered assumptions. For financial, electrical, agricultural or other consequential decisions, verify the result with the relevant qualified professional, supplier or official guidance.
