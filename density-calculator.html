Density Calculator

Find density from mass and volume, or solve for the missing mass or volume.

Formula

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

Example

A sample with mass 100 g and volume 20 cm³ has density 5 g/cm³.

Units

Keep mass and volume units consistent. The result uses the same mass unit per volume unit, such as g/cm³ or kg/m³.
