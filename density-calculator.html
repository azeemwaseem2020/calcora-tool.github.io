Density Calculator

Calculate density from mass and volume and understand the units used in the result.

Enter mass and volume

Density formula

Density = Mass ÷ Volume, often written as ρ = m/V.

Worked example

If a sample has a mass of 100 g and a volume of 20 cm³, its density is 100 ÷ 20 = 5 g/cm³.

Density units

Mass and volume units must be consistent. Common combinations include g/cm³, kg/m³ and g/mL. Since 1 mL equals 1 cm³, g/mL and g/cm³ are numerically equivalent when those units are used together.

How to use the calculator

  4. Check the displayed value and calculation steps.

Why density matters

Density helps compare materials, identify substances in laboratory work and understand how mass is distributed within a given volume.

Important limitation

This calculator performs the basic density relationship. It does not automatically convert incompatible units or account for changes in density caused by temperature, pressure or composition.
