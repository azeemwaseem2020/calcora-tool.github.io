SOLAR • PV PANEL SIZING

Solar Panel Calculator Pakistan

Estimate required PV capacity and the number of panels from daily energy demand and local planning assumptions.

How solar panel sizing works

A first-pass PV size is daily energy divided by peak sun hours and overall system efficiency. Panel count is the PV size multiplied by 1,000 and divided by panel wattage, rounded up to a whole panel.

Example

For 15 kWh/day, 5 peak sun hours and 80% overall efficiency, the estimated PV capacity is 3.75 kW. With 550 W modules, the result rounds up to 7 panels, or 3.85 kW nameplate capacity.

Pakistan planning notes

Actual production varies with shading, orientation, temperature, dust, wiring, inverter losses and seasonal sunlight. Use current site measurements and installer specifications for final system design.
