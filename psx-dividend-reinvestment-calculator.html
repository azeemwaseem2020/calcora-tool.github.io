PSX Dividend Reinvestment Calculator

Model how repeated dividend reinvestment could change share count and value when the dividend per share and reinvestment price are held constant.

Reinvestment assumptions

How the model works

Each period calculates dividend cash from the current share count, subtracts the entered withholding rate, then uses the remaining cash to buy additional shares at the entered reinvestment price. Fractional shares are allowed in the model so the compounding effect can be shown clearly.

Why reinvestment can matter

When dividends are reinvested, future dividends can be calculated on a larger share count. The effect depends on the actual dividend, share price, timing, taxes and whether dividends are paid consistently.

Important limitation

This is a scenario model, not a forecast. Dividend declarations and share prices change, and actual broker rules may not permit the exact fractional-share treatment shown here.
