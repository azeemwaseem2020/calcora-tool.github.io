Pythagorean Theorem Calculator

Find the hypotenuse or a missing leg of a right triangle.

Formula

a² + b² = c²

Example

With legs 3 and 4, c = √(3² + 4²) = 5.

Important

This calculator assumes a right triangle. For other triangles, use an appropriate geometry formula.
