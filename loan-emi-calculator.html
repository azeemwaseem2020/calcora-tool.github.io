How loan EMI is calculated

For a standard amortizing loan, the monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12 and the number of payments is the term in years multiplied by 12. The standard EMI formula is P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the financed principal.

Loan amount vs down payment

A down payment reduces the amount financed. Calcora therefore treats the entered loan amount as the purchase or requested amount and subtracts the optional down payment before calculating EMI.

What the results mean

Monthly EMI is the scheduled payment. Total interest is the estimated interest over the term. Total repayment includes principal and interest. Total cash cost adds the optional one-time fee and down payment to the repayment estimate.

Common loan calculator searches

People often compare a monthly payment calculator, personal loan EMI, home loan EMI, car loan EMI, loan interest calculator, amortization calculator and EMI calculator in Pakistan. The mathematics is similar, but the actual product rules can differ by lender.

Before using an EMI estimate

  • Check whether the quoted rate is fixed, variable or promotional.
  • Ask for processing, insurance and early-settlement charges.
  • Confirm whether interest is calculated monthly, daily or under another method.
  • Compare total repayment, not only the advertised EMI.
