The monthly EMI depends on the amount borrowed, interest rate and number of monthly payments. Use the result to compare scenarios, then confirm the final schedule with your lender.

Tip: A lower rate or shorter term can reduce total interest, but a shorter term usually means a higher monthly payment.

How the EMI calculation works

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the calculator converts the annual interest rate to a monthly rate and applies the standard EMI formula to the principal and total number of monthly payments.

EMI calculations can differ from a lender's final schedule when there are fees, insurance, changing rates, rounding rules or other loan conditions. Treat this result as a planning estimate.
