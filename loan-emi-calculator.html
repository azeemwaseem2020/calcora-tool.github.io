Loan EMI in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh

The same reducing-balance mathematics can be used for planning across countries, but the actual lender product is local. In Pakistan, borrowers may see terms such as markup, PKR, personal loan, car finance or home finance. In India, EMI searches commonly relate to personal, home and car loans and prepayment. In Bangladesh, users often search for monthly installment, BDT loan amount and bank-loan EMI. Use the calculator as an estimate and confirm the lender's own schedule.

How loan EMI is calculated

For a standard amortizing loan, the monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12 and the number of payments is the term in years multiplied by 12. The standard EMI formula is P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the financed principal, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments.

Loan amount, down payment and fees

A down payment reduces the amount financed. Processing fees and other one-time charges may increase the real cash cost without changing the mathematical EMI. Compare both the monthly payment and total repayment when evaluating an offer.

What changes your EMI?

  • Loan amount: borrowing more increases the monthly payment and total interest.
  • Rate: a higher rate increases both EMI and total interest.
  • Tenure: a longer term usually lowers the EMI but increases total interest.
  • Prepayment: an early principal payment can reduce future interest, depending on lender rules and charges.
  • Fees and insurance: these can raise the real cash cost even when they are outside the mathematical EMI.

Worked example: why tenure matters

Suppose the same financed amount and interest rate are offered over two different terms. The shorter term normally produces a higher monthly EMI but fewer interest-bearing periods. The longer term normally produces a lower EMI but more total interest. Use the calculator to compare the actual numbers rather than choosing from the monthly figure alone.

Before comparing a lender quote

  • Check whether the quoted rate is fixed, variable or promotional.
  • Ask for processing, insurance and early-settlement charges.
  • Confirm whether the lender uses reducing balance or another method.
  • Compare total repayment, not only the advertised monthly EMI.
  • For Islamic financing, check the institution's actual profit/rent structure and agreement rather than treating a generic EMI estimate as a contract quote.
