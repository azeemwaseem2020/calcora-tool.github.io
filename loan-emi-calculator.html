Know the real cost

EMI is based on the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. Use the result as a planning estimate, then confirm the final schedule with your lender.

Tip: A lower rate or shorter term can reduce total interest, although the monthly payment may change.

How the EMI calculation works

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the calculator applies the common EMI formula using the loan amount, annual rate converted to a monthly rate, and the total number of monthly payments.
