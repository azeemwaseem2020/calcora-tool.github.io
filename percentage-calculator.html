Percentage Calculator

Find a percentage of a number, calculate what percent one number is of another, or measure percentage increase and decrease.

Calculate X% of Y

Percentage formula

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Worked steps

  1. Divide the percentage by 100.
  2. Multiply the decimal by the number.
  3. The result is the requested percentage of the number.

Example

15% of 200 = (15 ÷ 100) × 200 = 30.

Common uses

Percentages are useful for discounts, markups, commissions, tips, tax estimates, exam scores and comparing changes. For regulated taxes or financial decisions, verify the applicable current rate separately.
