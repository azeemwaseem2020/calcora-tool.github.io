How to use the PSX toolset

  1. Choose the calculator that matches your search or calculation task.
  2. Enter broker-record prices, share quantities and actual transaction costs where supported.
  3. Separate price profit, dividends and corporate-action effects rather than combining unrelated assumptions.
  4. For multiple holdings, use the portfolio calculator to review allocation and unrealised performance.
  5. For additional purchases, use the average-price calculator to update weighted cost.
  6. Verify tax, brokerage and corporate-action treatment against current official information and broker records.

Core PSX calculations

Gross P/L = sale value − purchase value. Net P/L subtracts entered costs and tax assumptions. Weighted average price = total purchase cost ÷ total shares. Dividend income = dividend per share × shares. Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ reference share price × 100.

Why the tools are separated

Different PSX searches represent different jobs. A trader checking one transaction needs trade-level profit and break-even calculations; an investor reviewing several holdings needs portfolio allocation and return; an investor adding to an existing position needs weighted average cost. Keeping these intents on dedicated pages makes each tool easier to understand and reduces duplicated content.

Important limitations

Calcora does not provide live quotes, price targets or guaranteed returns. Brokerage, taxes and corporate-action treatment can vary. Verify current rules, company announcements and broker statements before relying on a calculation.
