PSX calculation workflow

  1. Record buy price, current/sell price, shares and real charges.
  2. Calculate gross and net P/L and break-even.
  3. Review return and holding-period context.
  4. Update average cost and portfolio allocation after new purchases.
  5. Review dividend income separately from price gain.
  6. Check bonus or rights issues against the actual company announcement.
  7. Reconcile estimates with broker records and current applicable rules.

Core formulas

Gross P/L = sale value − purchase value. Net P/L subtracts entered costs and tax assumptions. Weighted average price = total cost ÷ total shares. Dividend income = dividend per share × shares. Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ reference share price × 100.

Important limitations

Calcora does not provide live quotes, price targets or guaranteed returns. Brokerage, taxes and corporate-action treatment can vary. Verify current rules and your broker statement before making decisions.
