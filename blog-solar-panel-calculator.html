SOLAR PANEL SIZING GUIDE

Solar Panel Calculator: How Many Panels Do I Need?

The useful answer starts with electricity consumption, not the number of bedrooms or the size of the roof.

Quick method: estimate daily kWh, divide by peak sun hours and a realistic system-efficiency assumption to get a first-pass PV capacity, then divide watts by your chosen panel wattage and round up.

Solar panel formula

PV size (kW) ≈ daily energy (kWh) ÷ (peak sun hours × system efficiency).

Then panel count = required PV watts ÷ panel watts. For example, a 5,000 W target using 550 W panels needs 10 panels when rounded up. The final array can differ because real production depends on location, season, temperature, shade, orientation, wiring and equipment.

How many panels does your home need?

Use recent electricity bills to find monthly kWh, divide by the number of days for a daily estimate, and then use your local solar resource. A bill amount alone is less precise because tariffs can include fixed charges, taxes and different rates.

Roof space matters

Panel count is only half the question. Usable roof area must account for access paths, shade, mounting clearances, water tanks, parapets and other obstructions. A higher-wattage panel can reduce panel count, but electrical compatibility and cost still matter.

Why simple online estimates differ

Different calculators may assume different sun hours, losses, panel wattage and energy targets. Professional PV models can use location and system characteristics to estimate production rather than treating sunlight as a fixed number. Always compare the assumptions before comparing the result.

Pakistan-focused planning

For Pakistan, also consider seasonal cooling loads, daytime versus evening consumption, roof dust and the utility arrangement. If storage is needed, size the battery around the loads you actually want backed up, not automatically around the entire connected load.

Before buying panels

  • Check at least several months of kWh usage.
  • List high-load appliances and their running hours.
  • Measure usable, unshaded roof area.
  • Confirm panel wattage and inverter electrical limits.
  • Ask for a site-specific production estimate.
