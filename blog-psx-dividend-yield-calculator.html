PSX Dividend Yield Calculator Guide

Dividend yield is useful only when the dividend period and reference price are clear. This guide separates dividend income, yield on price and yield on cost.

Dividend income formula

Gross dividend income = dividend per share × number of shares. If you own 2,000 shares and the declared dividend is PKR 8 per share, gross dividend income is PKR 16,000.

Dividend yield formula

Dividend yield = annual dividend per share ÷ reference share price × 100. A PKR 10 annual dividend on a PKR 200 reference price gives a 5% yield.

Yield on cost

Yield on cost compares annual dividend per share with your own average purchase cost. It can be useful for portfolio review, but it should not be confused with the yield available to a new buyer at today's price.

Annual versus single dividend

A single interim or final dividend is not automatically an annual dividend. When comparing companies, use comparable periods and check the company's official announcement.

Net dividend

Net dividend can be estimated as gross dividend minus the withholding amount you enter. Do not assume a universal tax rate: applicable tax treatment can change and may depend on investor circumstances.

Why dividend yield can mislead

  • A falling share price can make a historical dividend yield look high.
  • A one-off dividend can distort an annual comparison.
  • Future dividends are not guaranteed.
  • Corporate actions can change the share count and cost basis.
  • Taxes can reduce cash actually received.

PSX investor workflow

  1. Verify the announced dividend per share.
  2. Confirm the dividend period.
  3. Enter actual shares held.
  4. Choose a clearly stated reference price.
  5. Calculate gross income and yield.
  6. Apply only verified tax assumptions when needed.

What Calcora does

Calcora performs the dividend arithmetic from your inputs. It does not predict future dividends, select dividend stocks or replace company announcements, broker records or professional advice.

FAQ

Is dividend yield the same as dividend income?

No. Income is the cash amount for your shares; yield is a percentage relating dividend per share to a reference price.

Can I calculate portfolio dividend yield?

Yes. Add the expected dividend income of each holding and compare it with the chosen portfolio cost or value basis.
