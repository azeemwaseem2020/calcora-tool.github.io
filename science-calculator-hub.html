Formula map

Density: ρ = m ÷ V. Force: F = m × a. Average velocity: v = displacement ÷ time. Acceleration: a = change in velocity ÷ time. Kinetic energy: KE = ½mv².

Unit discipline matters

Convert values into compatible SI units before applying formulas when the calculator expects SI input. A numerically correct formula can still produce a wrong answer when units are mixed.

Learning workflow

  1. Write down the known values and units.
  2. Choose the formula that connects the known and unknown quantities.
  3. Convert units if required.
  4. Calculate and inspect the magnitude.
  5. Check whether the result has the expected unit and direction/sign where relevant.
