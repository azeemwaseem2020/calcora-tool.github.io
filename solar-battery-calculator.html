Solar Battery Calculator Pakistan

Estimate the battery energy and amp-hour capacity needed for a selected backup load and duration.

Battery sizing formula

Required load energy is load in kW × backup hours. The nominal battery energy estimate divides that energy by usable depth of discharge and system efficiency. Approximate amp-hours = nominal watt-hours ÷ battery voltage.

Example

A 1,000 W load for 4 hours needs 4 kWh of delivered energy before losses. The calculator then adjusts for your usable depth of discharge and efficiency.

Important for Pakistan solar systems

Actual battery selection depends on battery chemistry, temperature, aging, manufacturer limits, surge loads and whether the inverter can use the battery safely. Use this page for planning, not as a final electrical design.
