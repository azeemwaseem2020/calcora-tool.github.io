What this PSX calculator answers

It covers common Pakistan Stock Exchange calculations: share profit or loss, percentage return, break-even price, dividend income and dividend yield. It is designed for a quick check of a position using your own average purchase price and current or sale price.

PSX profit and loss formula

Invested amount = average buy price × shares. Current or sale value = current price × shares. Gross profit or loss = current value − invested amount. Estimated net profit or loss = gross profit or loss − optional costs entered above.

PSX return percentage

The calculator expresses price return as gross profit or loss divided by invested amount × 100. It is not an annualized return and does not include every possible tax, fee or cash-flow effect.

PSX break-even price

With the optional costs entered, break-even price = (invested amount + costs) ÷ shares. This is a mathematical break-even based only on the costs you supplied.

PSX dividend calculator

Gross dividend income = dividend per share × shares. Dividend yield at the entered current or reference price = dividend per share ÷ price × 100. The amount actually received can differ because withholding, eligibility, corporate actions and investor circumstances can affect the cash distribution.

PSX research terms investors commonly use

When researching a listed company, investors may compare share price, percentage change, one-year change, market value, P/E ratio, dividend per share and dividend yield. These are research fields, not signals that a security is a good or bad investment.

Corporate actions and adjusted prices

Dividends, bonus issues and rights can affect the interpretation of historical prices and cost basis. For a detailed portfolio review, check official exchange announcements and your broker's transaction history instead of relying on a simple price difference.

Why Calcora does not show a live PSX price

Live market data needs a reliable data source and clear update rules. Rather than display stale or invented prices, this calculator asks you to enter the current or sale price you want to analyse. For current market information, verify the figure through an authoritative market source or your broker.
