PSX profit and loss formula

Invested amount = average buy price × shares. Current or sale value = current price × shares. Gross profit or loss = current value − invested amount. Estimated net profit or loss = gross profit or loss − optional trading costs.

Return percentage

Return percentage is measured against the invested amount. It is a simple price-return estimate and does not annualize the result or account for every tax and transaction charge.

Break-even share price

When optional costs are entered, break-even price = (invested amount + costs) ÷ shares. This is the price at which the position covers the entered costs, before any unentered taxes or charges.

Dividend income and yield

Gross dividend income = dividend per share × shares. Dividend yield at the entered current price = dividend per share ÷ current price × 100. Actual cash received can differ because applicable deductions and eligibility dates depend on the security and investor circumstances.

Corporate actions and historical prices

Dividends, bonus issues and rights can affect how historical prices should be interpreted. For serious analysis, use the official Pakistan Stock Exchange notices and your broker's records rather than assuming an unadjusted price series is directly comparable.

Planning checklist

  • Use the actual average purchase price from your trade history.
  • Include known brokerage and transaction costs where possible.
  • Check corporate actions when comparing old and current prices.
  • Do not treat a calculator output as a buy or sell recommendation.
