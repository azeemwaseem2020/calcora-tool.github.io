PSX profit and loss formula

Invested amount = average buy price × shares. Market value = current price × shares. Gross profit or loss = market value − invested amount. Estimated net profit or loss = gross profit or loss − optional costs.

Return percentage

Return percentage is calculated against the invested amount. Brokerage, taxes and other charges can change the final amount received.

Dividend yield and income

Dividend income = dividend per share × shares. Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ share price × 100. The Pakistan Stock Exchange describes dividend yield as cash dividend relative to a security's share price. citeturn0search2
