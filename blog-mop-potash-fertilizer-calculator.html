MOP Potash Fertilizer Calculator: How to Calculate 0-0-60

MOP is commonly used as a potassium source. The important calculation is to distinguish product weight from labelled K₂O supplied.

What does 0-0-60 mean?

A 0-0-60 fertilizer is labelled as 60% K₂O by weight and has no labelled N or P₂O₅. Confirm the actual guaranteed analysis before calculating.

Calculate K₂O supplied

K₂O supplied = MOP weight × 0.60

For example, 50 kg of 0-0-60 supplies 30 kg K₂O on the labelled basis.

Calculate MOP from a K₂O target

If a trusted recommendation requires 60 kg K₂O per selected area, a 60% K₂O product requires 60 ÷ 0.60 = 100 kg MOP mathematically.

Elemental K versus K₂O

Fertilizer labels use K₂O, while some technical recommendations may use elemental potassium. A commonly used conversion is K = K₂O × 0.83. Make sure the target and product use the same basis before calculating.

Calculate bags

Divide total MOP kilograms by the actual bag net weight. If purchasing whole bags, round the purchase quantity up while keeping the calculated field application separate.

Cost per kg K₂O

For nutrient economics, compare cost per unit of K₂O rather than only price per bag. Product cost per kg K₂O is approximately product price per kg ÷ 0.60 for a 60% K₂O product.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing K₂O with elemental K.
  • Using 60% when the actual product grade differs.
  • Mixing kg/acre and kg/ha.
  • Ignoring other potassium sources in the plan.
  • Assuming a potassium target without a suitable crop/soil recommendation.

FAQ

How much K₂O is in a 50 kg MOP bag?

At 60% K₂O, 50 × 0.60 = 30 kg K₂O.

How much MOP gives 50 kg K₂O?

50 ÷ 0.60 = about 83.33 kg product.

Is MOP suitable for every crop?

Product selection depends on crop, soil, salinity/chloride considerations and the nutrient recommendation. Use appropriate local agronomic guidance.
