DAP Fertilizer Calculator: How to Calculate 18-46-0

DAP is a compound fertilizer that can contribute both nitrogen and P₂O₅. This guide explains how to calculate its nutrient supply without double-counting nitrogen.

What does 18-46-0 mean?

A product labelled 18-46-0 contains 18% N and 46% P₂O₅ by weight, with 0% labelled K₂O. Verify the guaranteed analysis on the actual product label.

Calculate P₂O₅ from DAP

P₂O₅ supplied = DAP weight × 0.46

For example, 100 kg DAP supplies 46 kg P₂O₅ on the labelled basis.

Calculate nitrogen from DAP

N supplied = DAP weight × 0.18. A 50 kg bag therefore contains 9 kg labelled N when the analysis is 18-46-0.

Calculate DAP from a P₂O₅ target

If a trusted recommendation requires 46 kg P₂O₅ per selected area and the product is 46% P₂O₅, the mathematical DAP rate is 46 ÷ 0.46 = 100 kg per selected area.

Why DAP changes the remaining Urea requirement

If DAP already supplies part of the nitrogen target, calculate that contribution first. Only the remaining nitrogen should be supplied by another N fertilizer. This prevents overestimating the required Urea.

DAP bags and cost

Total bags = total DAP kilograms ÷ bag size. Cost = bags or kilograms multiplied by the applicable product price. Keep calculated field quantity separate from whole-bag purchasing quantity.

Acres versus hectares

Use the same area basis as the recommendation. A DAP rate in kg/ha should not be multiplied directly by an acreage figure.

Common mistakes

  • Counting DAP only as phosphorus and forgetting its N contribution.
  • Confusing P₂O₅ with elemental P.
  • Using an assumed grade instead of the actual label.
  • Mixing kg/acre and kg/ha.
  • Changing a recommended rate solely because of bag size.

FAQ

How much N is in a 50 kg bag of 18-46-0 DAP?

50 × 0.18 = 9 kg labelled N.

How much P₂O₅ is in a 50 kg bag?

50 × 0.46 = 23 kg P₂O₅.

Can DAP be combined with Urea in a fertilizer plan?

They can both appear in a nutrient plan, but the appropriate product combination and physical mixing/application method should be checked with current local guidance.

Does Calcora recommend DAP for my crop?

No. Use a crop- and soil-based recommendation, then use Calcora to perform the arithmetic.
