Force Calculator

Calculate force from mass and acceleration using Newton's second law.

Formula

F = m × a, where F is force in newtons (N), m is mass in kilograms (kg), and a is acceleration in metres per second squared (m/s²).

Worked example

For a 10 kg object accelerating at 2 m/s²: F = 10 × 2 = 20 N.

Calculation steps

  1. Enter mass in kilograms.
  2. Enter acceleration in m/s².
  3. Multiply mass by acceleration.
  4. Report the result in newtons.

Limitations

This is a basic Newtonian calculation. It does not model friction, drag, changing mass, relativistic effects or complex force systems.
