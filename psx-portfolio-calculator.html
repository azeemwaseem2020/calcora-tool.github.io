How the PSX portfolio calculator works

For each holding, invested cost is shares multiplied by average cost. Current value is shares multiplied by current price. Unrealised profit or loss is current value minus invested cost. Portfolio return is total unrealised profit or loss divided by total invested cost.

Portfolio allocation

Allocation shows how much of the entered portfolio value is represented by each holding. A high allocation to one security can make overall portfolio performance more sensitive to that security's price movement.

Dividend income and yield

Estimated annual dividend income is the sum of shares multiplied by the dividend per share entered for each holding. Portfolio dividend yield on cost is estimated dividend income divided by total invested cost.

Why this matters for PSX investors

PSX investor guidance highlights the importance of understanding transaction costs, dividends, dividend yield, company fundamentals and prudent portfolio decisions. Brokerage commissions can vary by firm, and investors should check their broker's tariff and applicable taxes rather than using a universal fee assumption.

Data source and limitations

Current prices are deliberately entered by the user. Calcora does not fabricate live market data. For current market research, use an authoritative market source and reconcile trade details with your broker statement. Corporate actions, taxes, charges and dividend declarations can change the actual result.
