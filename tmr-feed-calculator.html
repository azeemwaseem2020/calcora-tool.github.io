Important: this is quantity planning, not nutritional formulation. Animal class, body weight, production, forage analysis, dry matter, energy, protein, fibre and minerals may all matter.

TMR calculation formula

Ingredient quantity = total batch weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100. A 1,000 kg batch with an ingredient at 25% therefore contains 250 kg of that ingredient.

Dry matter versus as-fed weight

Dry-matter percentages and as-fed percentages are not automatically interchangeable. Fresh forage can contain substantial moisture, so physical loading weights may need to be converted using actual dry-matter content.

How to use a TMR calculator

Start with a formulation from a suitable feeding plan. Enter the intended batch size, number of animals and daily as-fed amount, then enter each ingredient's percentage. Check that the percentages total 100% and compare the calculated weights with your mixer capacity and scale readings.

Why a generic ration can be misleading

A ration that works for one herd or production target may be unsuitable for another. Milk yield, lactation stage, body condition, forage quality and laboratory analysis can change the nutritional requirement. Use Calcora for the arithmetic around a known formulation rather than treating a sample percentage mix as a universal feed recipe.
