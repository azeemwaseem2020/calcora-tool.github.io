Turn a percentage into kilograms

If an ingredient makes up a known percentage of the complete ration, this tool gives you the corresponding weight for your chosen batch size.

Important: this calculates quantity only. It does not create or validate a complete animal nutrition formulation.

How the TMR calculation works

Ingredient quantity equals target ration weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100. For example, a 200 kg ration containing an ingredient at 10% would require 20 kg of that ingredient.

A practical TMR also depends on the animal, forage and concentrate characteristics, dry-matter content and nutritional targets. Use this calculator for the arithmetic and rely on an appropriate feeding plan for the formulation.
