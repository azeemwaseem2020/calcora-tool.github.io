USE IT FOR THE ARITHMETIC

Check the mix before loading

Calcora converts the percentages you enter into kilograms and checks whether the batch is complete. It does not decide what a cow should be fed.

Important: nutrition is the hard part. Animal class, body weight, milk production, forage analysis, dry matter, energy, protein, fibre and minerals can all change a suitable ration. Use a qualified nutritionist or trusted formulation as the source of the percentages.

The TMR calculation

The arithmetic is direct: ingredient weight = batch weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100. For example, 250 kg from a 1,000 kg batch at 25% is 250 kg. The calculator repeats that calculation for each ingredient you enter.

As-fed weight and dry matter are different

A TMR may contain feeds with very different moisture levels. A percentage written on a dry-matter basis cannot simply be treated as the same physical loading weight on an as-fed basis. When a formulation is supplied on a dry-matter basis, convert it using the actual dry-matter percentage of each feed before loading.

Checking daily feed and batch coverage

If 20 animals each receive 20 kg of TMR per day, the herd uses 400 kg/day. A 1,000 kg batch would therefore cover about 2.5 days. This is a quantity check, not a recommendation for the feeding rate.

Why one sample ration is not universal

Dairy cows at different stages of lactation do not have identical requirements. Forage quality, body condition, production target and available ingredients also matter. A generic online recipe can look precise while being nutritionally wrong for a particular herd.

Mixing and scale checks

  • Confirm the mixer can safely handle the planned batch.
  • Use the actual moisture or dry-matter values when the formulation requires them.
  • Check each ingredient weight on the mixer scale rather than relying on memory.
  • Keep the same basis—dry matter or as-fed—throughout the calculation.
  • Recheck the formulation if an ingredient, forage lot or animal group changes.
