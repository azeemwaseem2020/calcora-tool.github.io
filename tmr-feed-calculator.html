TMR Feed Calculator

Calculate ingredient quantities from a target total mixed ration.

How the TMR calculation works

Ingredient quantity is calculated as target ration weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100. Use the percentage of the complete ration for each ingredient. Feed formulation should be checked by a qualified animal nutrition professional.
