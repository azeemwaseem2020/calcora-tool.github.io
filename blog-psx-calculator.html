PSX Calculator Guide: How to Calculate Profit, Loss, Return and Dividend Yield

A share price alone does not tell you what happened to an investment. Your purchase price, number of shares, selling price, costs and dividends all help complete the picture.

Quick idea: profit or loss comes from the difference between your sale/current value and your invested amount. Divide that gain or loss by the invested amount to get the basic percentage return.

What is a PSX calculator?

A PSX calculator is a planning tool for Pakistan Stock Exchange investors. Depending on the tool, it can estimate share profit or loss, return percentage, dividend income, dividend yield or a break-even price. Calcora's calculator keeps the inputs manual so you can use figures from your broker statement or market research without depending on a live data feed.

How to calculate share profit or loss

First calculate the invested amount: average buy price × number of shares. Then calculate the current or sale value: current price × number of shares. Gross profit or loss is the second number minus the first.

Example: Buy 1,000 shares at PKR 100 and the price later reaches PKR 120. The invested amount is PKR 100,000 and the market value is PKR 120,000, giving a gross gain of PKR 20,000 before costs.

Return percentage

Basic return percentage = profit or loss ÷ invested amount × 100. In the example above, PKR 20,000 ÷ PKR 100,000 × 100 = 20%.

This is a simple price-return calculation. Your real result can differ after brokerage, taxes, fees, dividends and corporate actions.

Break-even share price

If you know your total invested amount and additional costs, the break-even price is the amount you would need per share to recover those amounts: (invested amount + costs) ÷ shares. It is a useful way to see how transaction costs affect a position.

Dividend income

Dividend income is straightforward: dividend per share × number of shares. Dividend yield is commonly expressed as dividend per share ÷ share price × 100. PSX's investor guidance defines dividend yield as a measure of cash dividend relative to the share price. citeturn0search2

Why PSX corporate actions matter

Historical share prices can be affected by dividends, bonus shares and rights issues. PSX publishes its own methodology for ex-dividend, ex-bonus and ex-right price adjustments. PSX calculator checklist

1. Verify price

Use the correct buy, current or sale price and confirm the date.

2. Verify shares

Use the actual quantity held or sold rather than a rounded estimate.

3. Add costs

Include known brokerage or other charges when you want a closer estimate.

What this calculator does not do

It does not predict whether a stock will rise, choose a stock for you, fetch live prices or replace your broker's contract note. It also does not automatically apply every possible tax or regulatory rule. Those figures can change and may depend on the investor and transaction.

Use official market information

For current market information and corporate announcements, check the Pakistan Stock Exchange and your broker before making a decision. Calcora is designed to help with arithmetic and scenario planning, not to provide investment advice.
