PSX Capital Gain Calculator Guide

A share-price increase is only the starting point. A useful capital-gain calculation separates gross price gain, trading costs and any verified tax assumption.

Basic capital gain formula

Gross capital gain = (sale price − purchase price) × shares. If 1,000 shares move from PKR 100 to PKR 120, the gross gain is PKR 20,000 before costs.

Net trading result

Net result for a simple scenario = gross gain − entered buy-side costs − entered sell-side costs − any tax estimate you deliberately enter. Use actual broker charges where available.

Why CGT needs caution

Capital-gains tax is not a universal percentage that should be hard-coded into a calculator without context. Tax rules can change and treatment can depend on acquisition date, security, investor status and other factors. Calcora therefore treats tax as an input assumption rather than presenting an invented current rate.

Holding period and return

Holding-period return = net result ÷ invested amount × 100. An annualized return is a different calculation and can become misleading over very short periods.

Break-even

A simplified break-even sale price can be estimated by adding known costs and dividing the amount to recover by shares. Use your actual charges for a more useful estimate.

Common mistakes

  • Calling gross price movement the final profit.
  • Ignoring brokerage and other charges.
  • Using an unverified CGT rate.
  • Ignoring corporate actions that affect the position.
  • Using a current market price as though a sale actually occurred.

What Calcora does

The PSX calculator provides transparent arithmetic for profit, return, break-even, dividends and corporate-action scenarios. It does not file tax, predict prices or provide investment advice.

FAQ

Does capital gain include dividends?

No. Capital gain is the change in value of the share position. Dividends are a separate cash return and can be analysed separately.

Can I use this for a real tax filing?

No. Use current official tax rules and your broker records, and obtain professional advice when required.
