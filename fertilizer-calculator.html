Get the product quantity quickly

Once you know the field size and the recommended product rate, the arithmetic is straightforward: area multiplied by rate.

Good practice: follow the product label and local agronomic recommendations for the actual application rate. This tool does not choose the correct rate for your crop.

How the fertilizer calculation works

Fertilizer required equals field area in hectares × application rate in kilograms per hectare. For example, 2 hectares at 75 kg/ha gives an estimated 150 kg of product.

If your field is measured in another unit, convert the area to hectares before using this calculator. Product concentration, crop needs, soil conditions and local recommendations should be considered separately when deciding the appropriate rate.
