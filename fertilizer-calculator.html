Why this calculator goes beyond a basic rate calculator

A professional fertilizer workflow needs to connect field area, product analysis, nutrient targets, blend contributions and purchasing. Calcora keeps those layers visible instead of hiding them behind one recommendation number.

How the calculation works

For each product, nutrient supplied per selected area equals product rate multiplied by the nutrient percentage divided by 100. Total product equals rate multiplied by field area. In target mode, the planner uses a transparent sequential allocation and then reports the delivered nutrient balance.

N supplied = product kg × N% ÷ 100
P₂O₅ supplied = product kg × P₂O₅% ÷ 100
K₂O supplied = product kg × K₂O% ÷ 100

Why DAP nitrogen matters

DAP supplies both P₂O₅ and N. When a P₂O₅ target is met with DAP, its nitrogen contribution must be credited before calculating additional nitrogen fertilizer. The same principle applies to any product that supplies more than one nutrient.

Bags, leftovers and cost

Exact product mass is different from purchasing whole bags. Calcora shows exact bags and rounds the purchase quantity upward so the purchase decision is visible. Price is optional and is used only for arithmetic cost estimates.

Acres versus hectares

A kg/acre rate and a kg/ha rate are not interchangeable. Select the unit that matches your recommendation or explicitly convert it before calculation.

What the planner does not decide

  • It does not diagnose nutrient deficiencies.
  • It does not invent crop recommendations.
  • It does not guarantee yield, profit or nutrient response.
  • It does not know current local fertilizer prices unless you enter them.
  • It does not replace soil testing, product labels or qualified local guidance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate fertilizer per acre or hectare?

Yes. Select the matching area unit and enter rates or targets on that same basis.

Can I use multiple fertilizers?

Yes. Add up to four products. Target mode builds a transparent mathematical plan from the selected products.

Can a target be impossible?

Yes. The available products may not be able to cover every nutrient target without creating a different surplus or gap. Calcora reports the mismatch instead of hiding it.

Can I use custom grades?

Yes. Enter N, P₂O₅, K₂O and S exactly as shown on the actual product label.

Does it automatically choose the best fertilizer?

No. Product selection depends on crop, soil, documented nutrient requirements, timing, availability and local guidance. This page intentionally remains a calculation and planning aid.

Accuracy and safety note

Verify important rates against current soil information, crop-specific recommendations and the actual product label before application.
