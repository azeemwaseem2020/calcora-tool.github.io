More than just area × rate

See total product, approximate bags, cost and nutrient amounts supplied by the product grade you enter.

Important: the calculator does not choose a crop rate. Follow a current agronomic recommendation, soil test, product label and local guidance. A nutrient target and a product rate are different things.

How the fertilizer calculation works

For a product-rate recommendation, total product = field area × product rate. If a 5-hectare field receives 100 kg/ha, the estimate is 500 kg of product. With 50 kg bags, that is 10 bags.

Understanding N-P-K labels

A fertilizer grade such as 10-10-10 represents percentages of nitrogen (N), phosphate expressed as P₂O₅ and potash expressed as K₂O. The product quantity can then be multiplied by each percentage to estimate nutrient mass supplied. Do not confuse nutrient recommendations with product-rate recommendations.

Hectares and acres

Keep the area unit consistent with the rate. If a recommendation is in kg/ha, use hectares. If it is in kg/acre, use acres. Converting the field without converting the rate can produce a large error.

Bags and purchasing

Divide total product weight by bag size to estimate the number of bags. In practice you may need to round up to a whole bag, but confirm packaging, actual net weight and supplier terms before purchasing.

Cost estimate

Estimated product cost = number of bags × price per bag when a bag price is entered. This is only a purchasing estimate and excludes labour, transport, application and other farm costs.

What the fertilizer calculator cannot decide

The correct fertilizer and rate depend on crop, growth stage, expected yield, soil fertility, irrigation, nutrient removal, weather and local recommendations. A calculator should convert a trusted recommendation into quantities; it should not invent one.
