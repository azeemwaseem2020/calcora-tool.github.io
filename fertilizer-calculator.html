What this fertilizer calculator does

This tool answers the quantity question after a fertilizer rate is already known: how many kilograms, how many bags, how much N/P₂O₅/K₂O is supplied, and what the purchase cost would be at an entered bag price.

Fertilizer quantity formula

Total product = field area × application rate. A 5-hectare field at 100 kg/ha needs 500 kg. At 50 kg per bag, that is 10 bags.

NPK supply formula

N supplied = product weight × N%. The same calculation applies to P₂O₅ and K₂O. For example, 500 kg of 46-0-0 supplies 230 kg N. These are product-supply calculations, not crop recommendations.

Acres vs hectares

Always match the rate unit to the selected area unit. If your recommendation is 50 kg/acre, select acres. If it is 120 kg/ha, select hectares. Mixing these units can create a major purchasing error.

Fertilizer bags and cost

Divide total product by the package size to get the mathematical bag count. If whole bags must be purchased, round appropriately and confirm the final field plan. Cost = bag count × price per bag in this calculator. Actual farm cost may also include transport, labour and application.

Why DAP affects the nitrogen calculation

DAP commonly contains both nitrogen and P₂O₅. When a nutrient recommendation is being converted into multiple products, the nitrogen supplied by DAP must be accounted for before adding extra urea. Do not confuse calculation with recommendation

Calcora does not diagnose soil or prescribe a crop's nutrient requirement. A safe workflow is: obtain a current soil test or trusted crop recommendation → confirm the unit and nutrient basis → choose the actual fertilizer grade → calculate product quantity → plan application timing with local agronomic guidance.

FAQ

How many bags of fertilizer do I need per acre?

It depends on the recommended product rate and bag size. Enter the known kg/acre rate and field area to calculate total product and bags.

Can I use this for urea?

Yes. Enter Urea's actual label grade, commonly 46-0-0, and the known application rate.

Can I use this for DAP?

Yes. Enter the grade printed on the product. DAP supplies both nitrogen and P₂O₅, so its nutrient contribution matters when building a multi-product plan.

Does this calculator tell me how much fertilizer my crop needs?

No. It converts a known recommendation into quantities. Crop and soil recommendations should come from appropriate agronomic guidance and current soil information.

Can I calculate fertilizer cost?

Yes. Enter the price per bag. The calculator provides an estimate based on the mathematical bag quantity; actual purchasing may require whole bags and additional costs.

Important limitation

Fertilizer recommendations can affect crop performance, cost and environmental outcomes. Calcora is a calculation aid, not a soil-testing or agronomy service. Verify critical rates with a current soil test, crop-specific recommendation and qualified local agricultural adviser.
