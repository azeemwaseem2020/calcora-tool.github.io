Fertilizer Calculator per Acre vs Hectare

Area-unit errors are one of the easiest ways to turn correct fertilizer arithmetic into an incorrect field quantity.

Why acres and hectares matter

One hectare is approximately 2.471 acres. Therefore a rate expressed per hectare cannot be multiplied directly by an acreage figure.

Convert a hectare rate to an acre rate

kg/acre ≈ kg/ha ÷ 2.471

A 100 kg/ha rate is approximately 40.47 kg/acre.

Convert an acre rate to a hectare rate

kg/ha ≈ kg/acre × 2.471

A 40 kg/acre rate is approximately 98.84 kg/ha.

Calculate field quantity

After conversion, multiply area by rate using matching units. For example, 10 acres × 40 kg/acre = 400 kg.

Common mistakes

  • Multiplying acres by kg/ha.
  • Converting area but not the rate.
  • Rounding conversion factors too early.
  • Assuming local land units are interchangeable without checking their definition.

FAQ

How many acres are in a hectare?

Approximately 2.471 acres.

How do I convert 100 kg/ha to kg/acre?

Approximately 100 ÷ 2.471 = 40.47 kg/acre.

Can Calcora convert my recommendation?

Use the conversion logic here and then enter a consistent rate and area into the calculator. The tool does not decide the agronomic rate.
