How to use these guides

Use the calculator first when you need the arithmetic, then read the matching guide to understand how the result was produced. The guides focus on units, formulas, assumptions, limitations and practical checks rather than simply repeating keywords.

Topics covered

Finance guides cover EMI and compound interest. PSX content explains return, profit, loss and dividend calculations. Solar resources cover load, panels, batteries, inverters and system sizing. Agriculture guides cover TMR feed and fertilizer quantities.

What these guides do not replace

A calculator is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Financial decisions depend on current rates and lender terms; solar sizing depends on equipment specifications, site conditions and actual usage; agricultural decisions depend on crop, soil, animal and feed conditions. Verify important decisions with current information and qualified professional guidance where appropriate.

Calculation tip: Check the units before trusting a result. A correct formula can still produce a misleading answer when the input is in the wrong unit.
