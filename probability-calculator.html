Probability Calculator

Calculate the probability and percentage chance of a simple event from favourable and total equally likely outcomes.

Simple probability formula

P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total possible outcomes

To express probability as a percentage, multiply the probability by 100.

Worked example

For one favourable outcome out of six equally likely outcomes, P = 1 ÷ 6 = 0.1667, or about 16.67%.

How to use this probability calculator

  1. Enter the number of favourable outcomes.
  2. Enter the total number of possible outcomes.
  3. Select Calculate Probability.
  4. Review the decimal probability and percentage chance.

Important assumption

This simple formula assumes the outcomes are equally likely and that the favourable outcomes are counted correctly. It does not calculate conditional probability, combinations, permutations or dependent events.

Probability versus percentage

Probability is commonly written from 0 to 1, while the equivalent percentage runs from 0% to 100%. For example, 0.25 is the same as 25%.
