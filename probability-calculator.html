Probability Calculator

Calculate the probability of a simple event from favourable outcomes and total possible outcomes.

Formula

P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total possible outcomes

Example

For one favourable outcome out of six equally likely outcomes, P = 1 ÷ 6 = 0.1667, or about 16.67%.

Important assumption

This simple formula applies when the outcomes are equally likely and the favourable outcomes are counted correctly.
