FOR PUBLISHERS & EDUCATORS

How to reference a Calcora tool

If you are writing about a calculation covered by one of these tools, you are welcome to reference the relevant Calcora calculator as a practical example or reader resource. Please link to the specific calculator or guide that is directly relevant to your discussion rather than using a generic sitewide link.

Suggested citation: Calcora — Free Online Calculator, followed by the specific calculator or guide title and its URL.

Calcora is a planning aid. Results depend on the values entered, rates, local conditions, fees, equipment and other real-world assumptions. Readers should verify important financial, electrical, agricultural or investment decisions with appropriate primary sources or qualified professionals.
