Kinetic Energy Calculator

Calculate the kinetic energy of a moving object from its mass and velocity.

Formula

KE = ½mv², where m is mass in kilograms and v is velocity in metres per second. The result is joules (J).

Worked example

For a 10 kg object moving at 5 m/s: KE = ½ × 10 × 5² = 125 J.

Calculation steps

  1. Enter mass in kilograms.
  2. Enter velocity in m/s.
  3. Square the velocity.
  4. Multiply by mass and then by one-half.

Why velocity matters

Kinetic energy depends on the square of velocity. Doubling velocity therefore makes kinetic energy four times larger when mass stays constant.

Limitations

This formula is the classical kinetic-energy equation. It is intended for ordinary speeds where relativistic effects are negligible.
