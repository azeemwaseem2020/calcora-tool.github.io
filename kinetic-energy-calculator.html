Kinetic Energy Calculator

Calculate the kinetic energy of a moving object from its mass and velocity.

Enter mass and velocity

Kinetic energy formula

KE = ½mv², where m is mass in kilograms and v is velocity in metres per second. The result is measured in joules (J).

Worked example

For a 10 kg object moving at 5 m/s: KE = ½ × 10 × 5² = 125 J.

How to calculate kinetic energy

  1. Enter the object's mass in kilograms.
  2. Enter velocity in metres per second.
  3. Square the velocity.
  4. Multiply by mass and then by one-half.

Why velocity matters

Kinetic energy depends on the square of velocity. If mass stays constant, doubling velocity makes kinetic energy four times larger.

Units

Using kilograms and metres per second gives the result in joules. Convert other units first when necessary so the formula uses a consistent unit system.

Limitations

This uses the classical kinetic-energy equation and is intended for ordinary speeds where relativistic effects are negligible. It does not account for rotational energy or relativistic mechanics.
