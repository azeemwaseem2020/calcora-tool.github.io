PLANNING NOTE

Start with the energy, not just the panels

First estimate how much electricity your loads use in a day. Peak sun hours and expected system losses then give you a rough starting point for PV capacity.

Important: this is an estimate, not a final engineering design. Shading, roof direction, seasonal sun, battery backup, inverter limits and local conditions can change the required system size.

How the solar load estimate works

Daily energy is calculated as watts × hours ÷ 1,000. The starting PV capacity is then estimated by dividing daily energy by peak sun hours and the assumed system efficiency.

This simple approach is useful for an early estimate. A complete solar design should also consider the site's solar resource, panel specifications, inverter efficiency, battery needs, wiring losses and expected seasonal demand.
