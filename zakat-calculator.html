Pakistan Zakat Calculator 2026

Estimate Zakat on eligible assets. The default Nisab is set to PKR 503,529, the 2026 Punjab Zakat reference, and can be changed when your applicable authority or calculation method uses a different figure.

How the estimate works

  1. Add the eligible assets you want to include.
  2. Subtract the eligible liabilities allowed under your applicable Zakat rules.
  3. Compare the resulting net zakatable assets with the Nisab threshold.
  4. If the net amount reaches the Nisab, the calculator applies a 2.5% estimate.
Important: Zakat rules can depend on asset type, valuation date, ownership, debts and the scholarly or official method you follow. This tool is an educational estimate, not a religious ruling. The default Nisab is a Punjab 2026 reference; verify the current official Nisab and your applicable guidance before payment.
