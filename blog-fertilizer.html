How to Calculate Fertilizer Quantity

The basic calculation uses field area and the recommended application rate.

Basic formula

Multiply field area in hectares by the application rate in kilograms per hectare. For example, 2 hectares at 100 kg/ha requires an estimated 200 kg of product.

Use the recommended rate

The calculator does not determine the agronomic recommendation. Use the rate appropriate for your crop, soil and fertilizer product, and follow the product label and local agronomic advice.
