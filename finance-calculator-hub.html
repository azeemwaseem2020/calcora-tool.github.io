One finance workflow

  1. Define the financial question: payment, affordability, tax, Zakat or growth.
  2. Enter verified amounts and the correct time period.
  3. Review the formula and assumptions shown with the result.
  4. Compare scenarios rather than relying on one number.
  5. Include fees, taxes and other costs where the tool supports them.
  6. Check the result against the lender, employer, broker or current official rules when applicable.

Key formulas

For a standard amortizing loan, EMI depends on principal, periodic rate and number of payments. Compound growth depends on principal, rate, compounding frequency and time. Zakat estimates commonly apply the selected 2.5% rate to the entered net assessable amount; eligibility and Nisab treatment require appropriate current guidance.
