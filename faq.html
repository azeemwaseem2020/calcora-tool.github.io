QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

A quick reference for using Calcora and understanding what its results mean.

Are Calcora calculators free?

Yes. They are designed for free use without an account.

Are the results guaranteed to be exact?

No. Results are estimates based on the assumptions and inputs shown. Real-world rates, fees, losses and local conditions can change outcomes.

Can I use Calcora on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and designed for both smaller and larger screens.

Where can I learn the formulas?

Open Guides or use the guide link associated with a calculator when available.

Does Calcora send my calculator inputs to a server?

The calculators currently published are designed to perform their calculations in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for the site's current privacy approach.

What should I do if I find an error?

Please contact Calcora with the calculator name, inputs and expected result so the issue can be investigated.

Planning reminder: use important calculator results as estimates and verify consequential financial, electrical or agricultural decisions with appropriate current guidance.
