FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Calcora calculators free?

Yes. The calculators are designed for free use without an account.

Are the results guaranteed to be exact?

No. Results are estimates based on the assumptions and inputs entered. Real-world rates, losses, fees and local conditions can change outcomes.

Can I use Calcora on a phone?

Yes. The site is designed to adapt to mobile and desktop screens.

Where can I learn the formulas?

Open the Guides section or the guide linked from each calculator.
