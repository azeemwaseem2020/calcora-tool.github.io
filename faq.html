Frequently Asked Questions

Is Calcora free?

Yes. Calcora's public tools are designed to be free to use.

How does image resizing work?

The resize tool uses your browser's canvas functionality to create a new image at the dimensions you choose.

Are my images uploaded?

The current image resize page processes the selected image locally in your browser rather than sending it to a Calcora server.

What image formats are supported?

Common browser-supported formats such as JPG, PNG and WebP can be selected. The available output formats depend on browser support.
