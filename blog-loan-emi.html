Loan EMI Calculator Guide

EMI is the regular payment used to repay a loan over a chosen term.

How EMI is calculated

A standard reducing-balance EMI uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. A higher rate or longer repayment period can change both the monthly payment and total interest.

What to check

Compare the monthly EMI with your budget and look at total repayment, not only the monthly amount. Lender fees, taxes and other charges may not be included in a basic EMI calculation.
