HOW TO GET A BETTER RESULT

Calculate first. Understand second. Verify before deciding.

The result is only as good as the inputs. Keep units consistent with your bill, statement, product label or feed formulation. Compare scenarios when useful, and verify important financial, engineering or agricultural decisions with current authoritative information.

  1. Match the question to the right calculator.
  2. Use realistic inputs and correct units.
  3. Review the formula and assumptions behind the result.
  4. Compare scenarios instead of relying on one estimate.
