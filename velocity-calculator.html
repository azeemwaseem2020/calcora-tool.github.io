Velocity Calculator

Calculate average velocity from distance and time.

Formula

Average speed magnitude is calculated as v = d ÷ t, where distance d is in metres and time t is in seconds. The result is metres per second (m/s).

Worked example

For 100 m travelled in 10 s: v = 100 ÷ 10 = 10 m/s.

Calculation steps

  1. Enter the distance in metres.
  2. Enter elapsed time in seconds.
  3. Divide distance by time.
  4. Check that the units are consistent.

Important distinction

Velocity is a vector and includes direction. This simple tool calculates the average velocity magnitude from distance and elapsed time; it does not accept direction or changing motion.
