How to Calculate TMR Feed Quantities

TMR means total mixed ration: the complete feed mixture supplied to an animal or group.

The simple calculation

For one ingredient, multiply the target TMR weight by that ingredient's percentage and divide by 100. For example, 100 kg of TMR at 25% means 25 kg of that ingredient.

Important formulation note

A percentage calculation does not by itself create a nutritionally balanced ration. Nutrient requirements, dry matter, forage quality and other formulation factors should be reviewed by a qualified animal nutrition professional.
