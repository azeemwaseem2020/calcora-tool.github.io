TMR Feed Planning: Turning Ration Percentages Into Quantities

Once a target ration weight is known, a percentage can be translated into a practical ingredient quantity.

The simple calculation

Multiply the total TMR weight by the ingredient percentage and divide by 100. For example, 200 kg of total ration with an ingredient share of 25% corresponds to 50 kg of that ingredient.

Keep the unit consistent: if the total ration is entered in kilograms, the resulting ingredient quantity is also in kilograms.

Why the percentage is only one piece

A basic quantity calculation does not evaluate dry matter, nutrient density, animal class, intake, forage quality or the balance of the complete ration. Those decisions belong in a proper feed-formulation process.
