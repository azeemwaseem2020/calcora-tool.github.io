TMR Feed Calculator Guide: How to Turn a Ration Into a Real Batch

TMR means Total Mixed Ration: the ingredients are combined into a planned mixture so the intended ration is delivered consistently. The calculator handles the arithmetic; the nutritional formulation still needs appropriate feed information and professional judgment.

Core formula: ingredient quantity = total batch weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100.

What is a TMR?

A total mixed ration combines forage, concentrate and other ration components into one mix. The goal is to make the planned ration available consistently rather than allowing animals to select only certain ingredients. The exact recipe depends on the animal group and the nutritional targets.

How the TMR calculator works

Choose the batch size, enter the number of animals and daily feed amount, then add the ingredient percentages. Calcora converts each percentage into kilograms and estimates how long the batch would cover the entered daily feed demand.

Example: a 1,000 kg batch with an ingredient at 25% contains 250 kg of that ingredient. If 20 animals each consume 20 kg/day, the group uses 400 kg/day, so a 1,000 kg batch represents about 2.5 days of feed.

Why the 100% check matters

A complete percentage-based mix should total 100%. If your entries total 92%, 108% or another number, the ingredient weights do not represent a complete batch. The calculator flags this so you can correct the formulation before using the quantities.

Dry matter versus as-fed basis

This is one of the most important practical checks. A ration may be formulated on a dry-matter basis while ingredients are physically weighed as fed. A wet forage and a dry concentrate can have very different moisture contents. Converting a dry-matter target into as-fed quantities requires the actual dry-matter percentage of the feed.

What should be considered in a real dairy ration?

Body weight, milk production, lactation stage, dry-matter intake, forage quality, crude protein, energy, fibre, minerals, water and feed cost can all influence the ration. A generic percentage recipe should not be treated as a universal diet for every cow or herd.

TMR batch planning checklist

Check the formulation

Use a ration prepared for the correct animal group and production goal.

Check moisture

Confirm dry matter when moving between dry-matter and as-fed calculations.

Check the scale

Compare calculated weights with mixer capacity and actual weighing equipment.

Check the mix

Review ingredient order, mixing procedure and consistency according to your feeding plan.

Important limitation

Calcora's tool calculates quantities from the percentages you enter. It does not diagnose animals, analyse forage, calculate a complete nutrient balance or prescribe a feeding program. For a production ration, use current feed analysis and advice from a qualified animal nutrition professional or veterinarian where appropriate.
