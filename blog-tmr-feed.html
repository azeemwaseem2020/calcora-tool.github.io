TMR Feed Calculator Guide: Turning Ration Percentages Into Quantities

A total mixed ration is easier to batch when the percentage of each ingredient is translated into a clear weight.

Quick formula: ingredient quantity = total TMR weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100.

What is TMR?

TMR means Total Mixed Ration. In a TMR system, ingredients such as forages, concentrates and other ration components are combined so the intended mixture can be delivered as a batch. The exact formulation should come from the nutritional requirements of the animals and the feed materials being used.

How to calculate an ingredient quantity

If a batch weighs 500 kg and an ingredient represents 20% of the mix, the required quantity is 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100 kg. Keep the units consistent: a total ration entered in kilograms produces an ingredient result in kilograms.

Common TMR feed searches

Relevant searches include TMR feed calculator, TMR calculator, total mixed ration calculator, TMR ration calculator, feed ration calculator, TMR feed formulation and how to calculate TMR feed. These searches can cover anything from simple batch quantities to full nutritional formulation, so a quantity calculator should not be presented as a complete ration-formulation system.

Why percentages are only one part of feed planning

A percentage-to-weight calculation does not assess dry matter, crude protein, energy, fibre, minerals, intake, animal class, forage quality or other nutritional factors. Dairy cattle, beef cattle and other livestock can have different requirements. A qualified nutritionist or veterinarian should review a complete ration where appropriate.

Before feeding the batch

Check ingredient weights, moisture differences, scale accuracy and the final mix. If a formulation is expressed on a dry-matter basis, do not blindly convert it to as-fed quantities without accounting for moisture.
