TMR Feed Calculator Guide: How to Turn a Ration Into a Real Batch

TMR means Total Mixed Ration: ingredients are combined into a planned mixture so the intended ration can be delivered consistently. The calculator handles the arithmetic; the nutritional formulation still depends on appropriate feed information and professional judgment.

Core formula: ingredient quantity = total batch weight × ingredient percentage ÷ 100.

How the TMR calculation works

Choose the batch size, enter the number of animals and daily feed amount, then add the ingredient percentages. The calculator converts each percentage into kilograms and estimates how long the batch could cover the entered daily demand.

Worked example: a 1,000 kg batch with an ingredient at 25% contains 250 kg of that ingredient. If 20 animals each consume 20 kg/day, the group uses 400 kg/day, so the batch represents about 2.5 days of feed.

Why the 100% check matters

A percentage-based mix should total 100%. If the entries total 92% or 108%, the quantities do not represent the complete batch. Correct the formulation before relying on the calculated ingredient weights.

Dry matter versus as-fed weight

A ration can be formulated on a dry-matter basis while ingredients are physically weighed as fed. Wet forage and dry concentrate can contain very different amounts of water. Converting a dry-matter target into as-fed quantities requires the actual dry-matter percentage of each feed.

A useful dry-matter example

If a feed is 40% dry matter and you need 10 kg of dry matter from that ingredient, the approximate as-fed amount is 10 ÷ 0.40 = 25 kg. This is a calculation example only; laboratory or reliable feed-analysis data should be used for real ration formulation.

What affects a real dairy ration?

Animal body weight, milk production, lactation stage, dry-matter intake, forage quality, crude protein, energy, fibre, minerals, water and feed cost can all influence the ration. A generic percentage recipe should not be treated as a universal diet for every cow or herd.

Batch planning checklist

Formulation

Use a ration appropriate for the animal group and production goal.

Moisture

Confirm dry matter when converting between dry-matter and as-fed quantities.

Mixer capacity

Make sure the calculated batch fits the mixer and weighing equipment.

Consistency

Follow the planned ingredient order and mixing procedure.

Important limitation

Calcora calculates ingredient quantities from the percentages you enter. It does not diagnose animals, analyse forage, calculate a complete nutrient balance or prescribe a feeding program. For a production ration, use current feed analysis and advice from a qualified animal nutrition professional or veterinarian where appropriate.
