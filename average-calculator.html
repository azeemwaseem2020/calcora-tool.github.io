Average Calculator

Enter numbers separated by commas or spaces to calculate the arithmetic mean, total and count.

Average formula

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

Worked example

For 10, 20 and 30, the sum is 60 and the count is 3, so the average is 60 ÷ 3 = 20.

When to use an average

The arithmetic mean is useful when you want one representative value for a group of numbers. It is commonly used for test scores, prices, measurements, sales figures and other numerical data.

Average vs median

The mean can be affected by unusually high or low values. If your data is heavily skewed, comparing the average with the median can give a more useful picture.
