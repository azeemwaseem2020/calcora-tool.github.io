Average Calculator

Enter numbers separated by commas to calculate their arithmetic mean, total and count.

Formula

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

Worked example

For 10, 20 and 30: sum = 60, count = 3, average = 60 ÷ 3 = 20.

Important note

The arithmetic mean can be strongly affected by unusually large or small values. For skewed data, compare it with the median.
