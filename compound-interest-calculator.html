THE BIG IDEA

Separate deposits from growth

Regular contributions can become a major part of the final balance. This version shows total contributions separately so you can see what you put in versus what the assumed return adds.

Important: An investment return is not guaranteed. This is a mathematical projection, not a promise of future performance.

Compound interest formula

For an initial amount without contributions, A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt). With a contribution made at the end of each compounding period, the future value of the contributions is C × ((1 + r/n)^(nt) − 1) ÷ (r/n). When the rate is zero, the calculation reduces to the starting amount plus contributions.

What compound growth means

Compounding means earned interest or return is added to the balance and can itself participate in future growth. The effect becomes more noticeable as time increases, but the result depends entirely on the assumed rate and contribution pattern.

Common searches this calculator helps with

Common intents include compound interest calculator, investment growth calculator, savings growth calculator, future value calculator, monthly compound interest and compound interest with monthly deposits. Choose a compounding frequency that matches the mathematical assumption you want to test.

What this estimate does not include

  • Taxes, platform charges, management fees or transaction costs.
  • Inflation and changes in purchasing power.
  • Withdrawals, missed deposits or changing contribution amounts.
  • Variable investment returns and periods of loss.

Use the result responsibly

For a real savings or investment product, compare this projection with the institution's terms and actual fee structure. A higher assumed rate can produce a higher projection but also represents a different risk and is not automatically a better choice.
