HOW IT WORKS

Use a calculator, then understand the result

Start with the calculator that matches your planning task. Enter realistic values, review the assumptions, and then open the related guide to understand the formula and limitations. This helps you use a calculator as a planning aid rather than treating one estimate as a guaranteed real-world outcome.

  1. Choose the right tool: select finance, PSX, solar or agriculture.
  2. Enter your inputs: use the units and assumptions shown on the calculator.
  3. Review the result: check totals, rates, quantities and any warnings.
  4. Read the guide: learn how the calculation works and what can change the result.
