A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Calculate, check, then decide

A calculator is useful when its assumptions match the question you are trying to answer. Start with the closest tool, enter realistic values, review the units and warnings, and then read the linked guide if you need more context.

  1. Pick the tool: choose finance, PSX, solar or agriculture.
  2. Enter your figures: use the units shown beside each input.
  3. Check the result: look at totals, rates and quantities rather than only the headline number.
  4. Use context: compare the estimate with current rates, equipment specifications, local conditions or professional advice where appropriate.
