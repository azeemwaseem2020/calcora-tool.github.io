A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Calculate, verify, then decide

Calcora is designed to make the number easy to get and easy to understand. Use the closest tool, enter realistic figures, check the units and assumptions, then verify important decisions against current documents, market information, equipment specifications or professional advice.

  1. Identify the question. Payment, growth, return, system size or quantity.
  2. Choose the matching calculator. Avoid forcing a different tool to answer the wrong question.
  3. Enter realistic inputs. Use figures from your bill, statement, product label or formulation.
  4. Inspect the result. Review totals, assumptions and warnings.
  5. Verify important decisions. Treat estimates as decision support, not a substitute for authoritative information.
