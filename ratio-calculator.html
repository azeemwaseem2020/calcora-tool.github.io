Ratio Calculator

Simplify a ratio and compare two quantities using equivalent ratios.

Formula

Simplified ratio = divide both values by their greatest common factor.

Worked example

12:18 has GCF 6, so 12÷6 : 18÷6 = 2:3.

Common uses

Use ratios for recipes, mixtures, maps, screen dimensions, finance comparisons and quantity planning.
