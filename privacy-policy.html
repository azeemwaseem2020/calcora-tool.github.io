YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains Calcora's current approach to information collected through the website and its calculators.

Calculator inputs: the calculators currently published on Calcora are designed to perform calculations in your browser. Calculator values are not intentionally sent to Calcora simply to produce a result.

Information we may receive

Basic technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other services if such services are enabled. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Calcora, the details you include may be used to understand your message, respond to you and improve the website. Please avoid sending passwords, financial account credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Calcora does not need cookies for calculator calculations. If analytics or other optional services are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them before they are used.

Policy changes

Website functionality can change. When that happens, this page may be revised to keep the description of data handling current.
