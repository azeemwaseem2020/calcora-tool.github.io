Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calcora handles information when you use this website and its free calculators. We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary and to be clear about third-party services that may process information.

1. Calculator inputs

Calcora's calculators are primarily designed to perform calculations in your browser. Values such as loan amount, interest rate, share price, appliance load or feed inputs are used to produce the requested result. Do not enter passwords, bank account details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information into calculator fields.

2. Information you provide

If you contact Calcora by email or another available contact method, the information you include may be used to understand and respond to your request. Please avoid sending confidential information unless it is necessary for the request.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Calcora may use basic browser functionality needed for the site to work. Third-party services used on the site may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement, security or other service functions. You can usually manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

4. Analytics

If analytics services are enabled, they may process information such as pages visited, approximate device or browser information, referral sources and general usage patterns. This can help us understand which pages are useful and where the website needs improvement.

5. Advertising

Calcora may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising subject to their own policies and the choices available to users.

6. External links

Calcora may link to external websites, tools or resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of an external service before providing it with personal information.

7. Data security

Reasonable steps are taken to maintain the website, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting confidential information through ordinary website forms or email.

8. Children's privacy

Calcora is a general-purpose calculator website. It is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

9. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where available, use privacy or advertising controls offered by third-party services. You can also choose not to provide personal information when a feature does not require it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics, advertising or applicable requirements change. The review date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last checked.
