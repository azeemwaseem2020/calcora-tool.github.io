Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calcora handles information when you use this website and its free calculators. The aim is simple: collect as little information as reasonably necessary and be clear about services that may process data.

1. Calculator inputs

Calcora's calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. Values such as loan amount, interest rate, share price, appliance load or feed inputs are used to produce the requested result. Do not enter passwords, bank account details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information into calculator fields.

2. Information you provide

If you contact Calcora by email or another available contact method, the information you include in that message may be used to understand and respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Calcora itself may use basic browser functionality needed for the website to work. Third-party services used on the site may use cookies or similar technologies for functions such as advertising, measurement or security. Your browser settings can usually be used to manage or block cookies.

4. Analytics

If analytics services are enabled, they may collect information such as pages visited, approximate device or browser information, referral sources and general usage patterns. This information helps us understand which pages are useful and where the site can be improved.

5. Advertising

Calcora may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising according to their own policies and applicable settings.

6. External links

Calcora may link to external websites, tools or resources. Once you leave Calcora, the privacy practices of the destination website apply. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service before providing it with personal information.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its services maintained, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting confidential information through ordinary website forms or email.

8. Children's privacy

Calcora is a general-purpose calculator website and is not specifically directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from users.

9. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where supported, adjust advertising or privacy controls provided by third-party services. You can also choose not to provide personal information when using features that do not require it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics, advertising or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last reviewed.

Questions? If you have a privacy-related question, please use the Calcora contact page.
